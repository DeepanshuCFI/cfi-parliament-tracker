      Methodology & data

      Built on the record.
      Checked, cited, refreshed daily.

      How this was built — and checked

      Road safety is answered by more than one ministry, so we enumerated every question — directly from Parliament's own question APIs — put to the four that own it: Road Transport & Highways (roads, vehicles, enforcement), Health & Family Welfare (trauma centres, ambulances, accident-victim care), Heavy Industries (vehicle crash standards), and Housing & Urban Affairs (pedestrian and footpath safety in cities). From those, the audited baseline kept the 1,415 whose titles concern road safety across the 16th–18th Lok Sabhas and Rajya Sabha sessions 232–270 (July 2014 – April 2026); the tracker now adds each session's questions daily, bringing the set to {{TOTAL}} through {{DATA_THROUGH_MY}}.

      Every record was cross-checked question-by-question against Parliament's own question APIs — the audit found zero spurious records. Each carries its answering ministry, is tagged by topic, matched to the asking MP's official profile (party, state, constituency), and linked to the Government's answer PDF. Refreshed daily during sessions.

      Caveats

      • Selection is title-based: a question that raises road safety under an unrelated title isn't counted. Still treat totals as a near-complete floor, not an absolute ceiling.
      • Only questions admitted and answered appear in the parliamentary record. MPs file far more question notices than the Houses can take up — admission is decided by ballot — so an MP may have submitted road-safety questions that were never drawn. One more reason to read a low count as a floor, not a verdict.
      • {{WITH_PDF}} of {{TOTAL}} records link to an answer PDF; a few older answers survive only in legacy formats.
      • Questions asked jointly are credited to every co-asking MP. MP names are matched to the official roster; a handful of low-confidence matches are flagged in the dataset.
      • Accuracy. Everything here is compiled by automated pipelines from the official records of the Lok Sabha and Rajya Sabha published on sansad.in, and checked continuously — but discrepancies, in the source records or in our matching, are possible. We present a good-faith reflection of the official record, not a claim of perfection, and no count here should by itself be read as a judgment of any MP. Spotted an error? Write to info@crashfreeindia.org (or report it on GitHub) — verified corrections ship in the next daily refresh.
