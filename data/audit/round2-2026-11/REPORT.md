# Round 2: Rajya Sabha audit and the borderline groups (23 Nov 2026)

**Status: applied on the branch `audit-round2` only. Not deployed. Awaiting the editor's review.**

## What is applied on the branch

- **Rajya Sabha, clear misses: 92.** Titles in scope under the existing rule that the baseline keyword list missed.
- **Rajya Sabha, borderline families accepted: 14.**
- **Lok Sabha, borderline families accepted: 58** (includes 4 Urban Affairs questions never pulled because the ministry's code changed).

Accepted borderline families, on the reasoning that each is 'associated with road safety' under the approved inclusive scope or follows a ruling the editor gave on 23 Nov 2026: traffic management and ITS systems on highways; highway helplines and incident services; street lighting on highways (visibility, as with dividers); driver qualification rules (licensing); third-party motor insurance (crash compensation); vehicle and EV fires (vehicle safety); ambulance and life-support training (post-crash care); medians, dividers and rail crossings (road engineering); and 13 single questions.

## Left out, for the editor to rule on

| Family | Lok Sabha | Rajya Sabha | Why it is not obvious |
|---|---|---|---|
| Bridge and tunnel collapses, structural defects | 19 | 8 | Road users are at risk, but many of these are construction-quality questions with no road-user angle in the title |
| Encroachment on highways and roadsides | 9 | 9 | The editor ruled footpath encroachment in; highway-land encroachment is a different thing |
| Single questions | 8 | 6 | Women's security in transport, sun-control film, general emergency numbers, air-conditioned truck cabins, parking fines and similar |

Also untouched: the 313 Lok Sabha questions where only the question text raises safety. Including them would change the method from title-based to text-based.
